I am an M.S. student in the Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering at Stanford University. I received my B.S. in Civil Engineering (minor in Finance) from Tongji University and spent a year as a visiting student at UC Berkeley.

I work on physics-based modeling and machine learning for engineered and infrastructure systems. Coming from civil engineering, I am drawn to problems where a physical model is known but expensive, incomplete or hard to act on, and where learning can close the gap: surrogates that make finite-element simulation fast enough for inversion, spectral physics paired with transformers, navigation policies that transfer from simulation to real robots, and LLM agents and offline reinforcement learning for decision-making.

Highlights

  • Built a neural-operator surrogate for a coupled poromechanics model of gas-field subsidence, trained on 1,000 finite-element simulations; it replaces a ~90-minute simulation with a millisecond prediction and makes Bayesian inversion against field leveling data practical.
  • Led HeuriGuide (first author, under review at ICLR 2027), which asks how much decision authority an LLM agent should have inside test-time optimization, evaluated on nine combinatorial optimization problems.
  • Designed a physics-informed Line-Set Transformer that identifies atomic species in hypersonic shock-tunnel spectra, combining a forward absorption model with learned line detection.
  • Diagnosed why quadruped navigation policies fail to transfer to a taller robot, traced it to how the terrain map is referenced to the body, and recovered the policies without retraining.
  • Co-authored NeighborDrive, which received the Best Paper Award (2nd Place) at SocialSys 2026; I developed its privacy-preserving vehicle detection on event images.
  • Team leader on an earthworm-inspired burrowing robot (granted Chinese invention patent; 1st Prize, Shanghai “Creation Cup”), from mechanical design to soil penetration tests.

Research Interests

Physics-Informed Scientific Machine LearningNeural operators and surrogate models that make high-fidelity simulation fast enough for inversion and uncertainty quantification, in subsurface mechanics, battery degradation, spectroscopy and wind power.
Robot Learning & Sim-to-Real TransferNavigation policies trained in simulation that hold up on real robots, and understanding why transfer breaks down across robots and terrain.
Learning-Based Decision-Making & OptimizationLLM agents for heuristic design, offline reinforcement learning with careful off-policy evaluation, and search for combinatorial problems.
Sensing & Perception for InfrastructureVision and mobile sensing that turn video, point clouds and sensor data from cities and construction sites into engineering information.
